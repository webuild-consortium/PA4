# MVP BU1 – Scenario 2 Tracking

## 1. Purpose

Central entry point for scenario onboarding, partner-specific preparation, MVP execution, and results tracking.
Each partner maintains its own tracker, while this README provides the consolidated overview.

## 2. Getting Started

1. **Review generic guidelines:** Read [WP4/README.md](../wp4/readme.md) for the common onboarding and tracking process.
2. **Review scenario requirements:** Check [Scenario 2 Template](MVP_PA4_Scenario2_Template_Partnername_Roles.md) for applicable roles, prerequisites, mandatory stages, and evidence requirements.
3. **Create your partner tracker:** Copy `MVP_PA4_Scenario2_Template_Partnername_Roles.md` and rename it according to your organization and technical roles.
4. **Register your tracker:** Add it to the Results Tracking table below, including owner, last update, and status.
5. **Complete Stages 1–8:** Execute applicable preparation activities and document status, evidence, and open issues in your tracker.

## 3. How to Run the MVP

1. **Stage 9 – Execute:** Define and run the applicable MVP flows, including successful and failure scenarios.
2. **Track results:** Record test cases, execution dates, expected and actual outcomes, evidence, defects, and retests.
3. **Validate completion:** Review results against acceptance criteria and document sign-off.

**Important:** Clearly identify missing evidence and unconfirmed requirements. Reported completion must not be treated as verified without supporting evidence.
